By April 2020, every RSM tax office in the country was racing to identify which of their clients qualified for CARES Act subsidies. They were all doing it manually. They were all doing it differently. They were all running out of time.

## The bottleneck wasn't the math

The tax leaders knew how to calculate eligibility — entity-type nuances, subsidy formulas, all of it. What they needed was a way to run that calculation once and have it apply across the firm, instead of 90 offices reinventing the same workflow in parallel.

The opportunity was time-boxed: millions of dollars per client, 20,000 accounts firm-wide, and a closing relief window. The firm couldn't capture it 90 times in parallel. It needed to capture it once.

## How it shipped

Worked it as an Innovation Lab project, in three moves:

![From tax codes to targeted relief — Dig, Translate, Size](/images/projects/cares-act-data-diagram.png)

1. **Sat with tax leadership** to learn the actual eligibility math — entity types, subsidy tiers, edge cases. This was their expertise; my job was to encode it, not invent it.
2. **Built Alteryx pipelines** that handled the entity-type nuances and could run against any office's client data.
3. **Started small.** The harder problem wasn't the pipeline — it was getting offices to standardize their data inputs (their old software had poor reporting, so most of it had to be manually keyed). Demonstrated a quick win in one office, then scaled to 90.
4. **Built Power BI dashboards** for tax leaders to explore the results and target the highest-impact upsell opportunities themselves.

## What it added up to

- **$200M of economic impact identified** across 20,000 client accounts
- **~10,000 hours of manual interpretation work** absorbed by the shared system
- **90 offices** standardized on the same calc — instead of running 90 versions
- The Innovation Lab got firm-wide recognition for positioning RSM as a proactive advisor during an international crisis

## What I took from it

The work that scaled $200M wasn't the Alteryx flow. It was convincing 90 offices to run their data through one shared spec — earning trust one office at a time, starting with the smallest visible win.

Most "data projects" are change-management projects in a hoodie. The pipeline is the easy part.
