## The Problem

The CARES Act made billions in relief available overnight — but reaching eligible businesses required answering a question the data couldn't answer directly: *who qualifies, and for how much?*

Eligibility lived inside tax codes — Form 941 line items, NAICS classifications, PPP forgiveness flags, EIDL status — spread across four legacy systems that didn't speak to each other. The interpretation work, translating regulatory language into a yes/no per business and then a dollar value, was the bottleneck. Compute was cheap; judgment was scarce.

Manual at this scale would have taken weeks. Eligible businesses would have aged out of the relief window before being contacted.

## The Approach

Worked the problem in three phases.

**1 · Dig.** Pulled and reconciled records from four legacy systems — IRS filings, state UI claims, business license registries, SBA loan flags. Used Alteryx for ingestion and dedupe; the goal at this stage was a single 100K+ candidate pool with normalized identifiers, not a clean answer.

**2 · Translate.** This is where the consulting work lived. Built rule mappings that turned cryptic tax-code patterns into plain-English eligibility verdicts:

- `NAICS 722511 + W2 < 500` → full-service restaurant, qualifies for ERC
- `Sched C gross < 50% YoY` → significant decline test passed, Round 2 PPP eligible
- `1099-NEC contractors > 0` → eligible for SETC

Each rule was defensible against the underlying statute, not just keyword-matched. 100K+ candidates collapsed to 20,000 verified eligible accounts.

**3 · Size.** Tiered the eligible accounts by expected relief value so administrators could sequence outreach by impact-per-contact:

- Tier A (high-value): 2,000 accounts × ~$20K = $40M
- Tier B (mid): 8,000 × ~$12K = $96M
- Tier C (long-tail): 10,000 × ~$6.4K = $64M

Total: $200M of addressable opportunity. Power BI dashboards gave program admins real-time visibility into which tier was being worked, the audit trail behind each verdict, and which accounts had been contacted.

## The Results

- **20,000 eligible accounts** verified against statute, not just matched against keywords
- **$200M in addressable economic opportunity** sized by tier — sequenced for impact, not just totalled
- **~10,000 hours of manual interpretation work** absorbed by the scoring framework
- Delivered fast enough that relief reached eligible businesses inside the program window
- Reusable methodology — the interpretation rules apply to any future relief program; the data plumbing reruns when fresh records arrive
